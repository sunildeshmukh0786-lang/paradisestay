About Paradise Stay

We help families, friends and teams find a private villa in Goa without the back-and-forth.

Paradise Stay lists 22 villas across 7 areas of Goa, from 1 to 7 bedrooms. Every villa shows its weekday and weekend price upfront, along with photos, house rules and the cancellation policy.

Each villa is booked as a whole, so the pool, the kitchen and the garden are yours for the stay. Tell us your dates and group size on WhatsApp and we'll confirm availability, the final price and payment details in the same chat.
